# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (beta)

First public version, built for WoW: Forever (Interface 16001) and tested with Attune 16001-34.

- Flat window in the active theme: header with the switch between attunements and results, settings and close; status
  bar, survey menu and resize grip in the same style; window scale 70–130 %.
- List with section headings, icons, level ranges and progress; Attune's icon grid hidden (optional).
- Chain cards with status strips, modern or classic status colours, three line weights, head card with the dungeon
  picture (banner, background or off).
- Zoom bar from 10 to 150 %, fit the whole chain, Ctrl + mouse wheel, remembered between sessions.
- Search with filters for attunements, quests, NPCs, items and zones; IDs and short names; keyboard control; jump to the
  step with a highlight; marks in the open chain.
- Quest detail panel, rewards, quest giver hint and results table in the same style.
- Settings window (Appearance, Search & zoom, Modules, Info) and, with EllesmereUI, the section "Attune Skin" in its
  options window; minimap button and addon compartment entry.
- Seven themes, accent colours, bundled fonts; readable light themes without text shadows.
- English and German.
